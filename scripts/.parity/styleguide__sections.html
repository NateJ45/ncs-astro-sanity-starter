This is the privacy policy for Studio Starter. The goal here is to be straightforward about what information is collected and why.

What gets collected

When you fill out the contact form on this site, you share your name, email address, and whatever details you include in your message. That information is used only to respond to you and to help plan your project. Nothing else.

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address goes to the email marketing provider so occasional news and updates can reach you. It is not shared with anyone else.

What doesn't happen

  • Your information is never sold.
  • There is no ad targeting on this site.

How traffic is measured

This site runs no analytics at all. Visits are not counted, and nothing is stored on your device for measurement.

Unsubscribing from the newsletter

Every email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you're off the list right away. If you run into any trouble, email directly and it'll be handled promptly.

Questions or data requests

If you have questions about how your information is handled, or want your data removed, reach out at hello@example.com. You'll hear back within a few business days.

This policy was last reviewed in May 2026 and will be updated if anything meaningful changes about how this site handles your data.
